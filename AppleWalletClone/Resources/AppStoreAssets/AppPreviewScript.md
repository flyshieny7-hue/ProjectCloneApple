# App Preview Video Script
## Apple Wallet Clone - 30 seconds

### Scene 1: Welcome (0:00 - 0:05)
- **Visual**: App launches, dark gradient background appears
- **Cards**: 3 credit cards animate in with spring physics
- **Text Overlay**: "All your cards in one place"
- **Audio**: Soft chime, upbeat music starts

### Scene 2: Quick Pay (0:05 - 0:10)
- **Visual**: User taps a card, NFC animation plays
- **Effect**: Haptic feedback visual, checkmark appears
- **Text Overlay**: "Tap to pay instantly"
- **Transition**: Smooth zoom into card detail

### Scene 3: Budget Tracking (0:10 - 0:18)
- **Visual**: Animated pie chart segments appear
- **Data**: Sample spending categories (Food, Transport, Shopping)
- **Text Overlay**: "Track every dollar"
- **Effect**: Numbers count up animation

### Scene 4: Security (0:18 - 0:25)
- **Visual**: Face ID scan animation, lock icon
- **Effect**: Shield appears around transaction
- **Text Overlay**: "Bank-grade security"
- **Transition**: Fade to settings screen

### Scene 5: Customization (0:25 - 0:30)
- **Visual**: Settings menu scrolls, dark mode toggle
- **Effect**: Theme changes in real-time
- **Text Overlay**: "Your wallet, your way"
- **End**: App icon with tagline

### Technical Specs
- **Resolution**: 1080x1920 (portrait)
- **Frame Rate**: 60fps
- **Format**: H.264, MP4
- **Max Size**: 500MB
- **Music**: Licensed upbeat electronic track
- **Text**: San Francisco font, white with subtle shadow
